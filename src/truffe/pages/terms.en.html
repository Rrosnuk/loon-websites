Not veterinary advice

Truffe organizes information you give it and reminds you about dates. It does not diagnose, treat or recommend care. Always follow your veterinarian’s guidance. In an emergency, call your vet or the nearest emergency animal hospital; do not rely on the app.

Check what the scanner reads

Truffe reads printed and handwritten papers on your phone. It can misread a word, a date or a number, and it asks when a date could mean two things. You are responsible for checking each field before saving, and for confirming important dates, vaccines and doses against the original paper or with your vet.

Reminders

Reminders depend on your phone, its notification settings, battery and the dates you entered. A missed or late notification is possible. Treat reminders as a helpful backup, not the only way you track your pet’s care.

Records, photos and documents you add belong to you and stay on your device. You can export them at any time. Only add documents you have the right to keep.
