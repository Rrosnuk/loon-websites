From paper to reminders in a minute

Truffe does the typing, so your pets’ records are complete without the chore.

Scanning

It reads the paper. You check it. It’s saved.

Point the camera and Truffe finds the page, reads the vaccines, lot numbers, microchip, medicines and invoice, and shows you every field before it saves. The original is kept.

When a date could be read two ways, like 03/04/2026, Truffe asks instead of guessing, then remembers how each clinic writes them.

Reminders

Reminders that follow your pet’s real life

  • Vaccines, flea and tick, heartworm and medicines, counted from the day you actually gave them
  • City licence renewal, with a note on how licensing works where you live
  • A reaction log to show your vet
  • A one-page vaccine card PDF for the groomer or kennel

Costs

Know what each pet really costs

Scan an invoice and its items go into that pet’s costs, with tax on its own line. See totals by year, by category and by pet, in your own currency.

Questions

When can I get Truffe?

Truffe is being prepared for the App Store for iPhone. An Android version is planned. Email us and we’ll tell you the day it’s out.

Which pets does it support?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, reptiles and horses, with as many pets as you like in one app.

Does it work with my vet?

Yes. Truffe reads the paper itself, so it works with any clinic, shelter or city licence office, in English or French, without your vet signing up for anything.

Is this veterinary advice?

No. Truffe keeps records and reminders. Always follow your veterinarian’s guidance, and in an emergency call your vet or the nearest emergency animal hospital.
