How we build

The same promises in every app.

Private by design

No account and no tracking. Documents are read on your device, so there is no server holding a copy of your life.

One job, done well

Each app solves a specific annoyance completely instead of doing a little of everything.

Made for where you live

Your language, currency, units and date format, starting with English and French and growing from there.

Your data, your exit

Export everything at any time. The free version is genuinely useful, and we never hold your data hostage.
