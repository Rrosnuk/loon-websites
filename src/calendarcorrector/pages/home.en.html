Questions

Does it change my original calendar?

No. Your original subscription keeps syncing untouched. Calendar Corrector writes a corrected copy into a calendar it creates, and you hide the original in the Calendar app.

Which calendars work?

Any calendar already on your device, or any calendar link (.ics or webcal) from a scheduling app, school, team or Google Calendar.
