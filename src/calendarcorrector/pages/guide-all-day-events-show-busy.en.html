Make all-day events show as busy instead of free

A colleague booked a meeting on your day off. Your calendar said you were free, because all-day events count as free.

Why it happens

Calendar apps treat all-day events as free by default, so a holiday, a PA day or a vacation from a subscribed calendar doesn't block your time. Booking tools and colleagues checking your availability see an open day.

Fix it for every event

  1. Add the calendar to Calendar Corrector.
  2. It notices and asks: "26 all-day events show you as free. Mark them busy?" Tap Fix.

To be choosy, open one event, set Show as: Busy, and apply it to all events with the same word, such as every "PA Day" or "Vacation".
