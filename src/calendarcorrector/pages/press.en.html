Short description (25 words)

Calendar Corrector fixes the calendars you subscribe to but can’t edit, like work shifts, school and sports schedules, and keeps a clean, auto-updating copy.

Medium description (75 words)

Subscribed calendars are read-only, so a shift that ends at 6 AM shows up on the wrong day and a school calendar fills up with every grade. Calendar Corrector reads those calendars, applies your fixes, and keeps a corrected copy in your own calendar that updates itself. Fix a title, an end time or a colour once and every future event follows. If a feed breaks, you keep the last good version. No account, no tracking.

What’s different

  • Fixes once, forever. Rules apply to every future event in the feed, not just the one you edited.
  • Overnight shifts done right. Shifts that end after midnight stay on the day they start.
  • Hide what isn’t yours. Other grades, other teams, other provinces’ holidays.
  • Never goes blank. If a feed breaks or suddenly empties, the copy keeps the last good version and warns you.
  • Private by design. Calendar links stay in the device keychain; nothing is sent to the developer.

About the developer
