Sports team calendar: arrive early and colour away games

The team calendar says the game starts at 7:00. The coach wants everyone there at 6:30, and away games are an hour's drive.

What to fix

  • Arrival time. Start every game 30 or 45 minutes early, so your calendar shows when to be there.
  • Location. Add the arena or field address so Calendar can show a map and tell you when to leave.
  • Away games. Put them in their own colour: Calendar Corrector moves them to a separate calendar such as "Hawks · Away games".
  • Alerts. Add a reminder the night before for early games.

How

  1. Add the team calendar to Calendar Corrector (pick it from the list or paste the link from your team app or league site).
  2. Open a game, change the start, location or colour, and apply it to All events with "Game", or with "Away", or whatever word the team uses.

New games get the same treatment as the schedule is updated.
